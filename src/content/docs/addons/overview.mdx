An overview of official and community-developed addons for [CouchCMS](https://www.couchcms.com), including links to their respective forum threads.

## Official Addons (included with CouchCMS)

These addons are bundled with CouchCMS (since v2.3) and are available in the `addons/` directory.

- Shopping Cart  
- Extended Comments  
- Extended Folders  
- Extended Users  
- Inline Editor  
- JCropThumb  
- Custom Routes  
- Page Builder  
- Bootstrap Grid  
- Data-Bound Form  
- Mosaic  
- NicEdit  
- PHPMailer  
- reCAPTCHA  
- Relation  
- Repeatable Regions
